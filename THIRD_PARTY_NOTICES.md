## Valheim WebMap

The world map follows the approach of [valheim-webmap](https://github.com/h0tw1r3/valheim-webmap)
(originally by Kyle Paulsen, MIT): the map is drawn on the server from the world generator and
explored areas are tracked from player positions. The biome palette is based on its colours.
No source code is copied.
